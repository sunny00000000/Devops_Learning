Billinger Bot v2.8.0 — Final Dark Accessibility Edition

This final UI stabilization release preserves the complete v2.7 learning, readiness, AI, career, portfolio, institute, backup and security system while replacing the light studio palette with a dark high-contrast accessibility design.

New display controls:
- Comfort Dark (default): lower glare, softer accents, reduced glow and motion.
- Standard Dark: deeper high-contrast dark presentation.
- High Contrast: increases body/secondary text contrast.
- Text Size: normal, large, or extra large.
- Reduce Motion: suppresses decorative animation/glow and preserves functional transitions.
- Quick Comfort toggle in the top bar.

Preferences are stored only in the local browser using localStorage. They do not alter student records or leave the laptop.
