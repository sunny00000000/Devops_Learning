# Billinger Bot v2.8.0 — Dark Accessibility Guide

## Default
The interface starts in **Comfort Dark** with **High Contrast** and **Reduce Motion** enabled. This is intended for long study sessions and lower perceived glare.

## Display controls
Open **System & AI → Accessibility & Display** to choose:
- Comfort Dark
- Standard Dark
- Normal, Large, or Extra-large text
- High Contrast
- Reduce Motion and Glow

The top-bar **Comfort** button switches between Comfort Dark and Standard Dark instantly. Preferences are stored in the browser's local storage only.

## Accessibility behavior
- High-contrast foreground text on all dashboard surfaces.
- Dark forms, cards, dialogs, tables, provider cards, readiness cards, and learning pages.
- Bright cyan focus rings for keyboard navigation.
- Reduced decorative motion and glow in Comfort mode.
- Dark scrollbars and selection colors.
- PDF and generated resume documents may retain their document-native colors because they are separate content artifacts.
