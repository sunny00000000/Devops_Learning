# Career and Portfolio Guide

## 1. Create the verified candidate profile

Open **Career Command → Candidate Profile**. Enter only facts you can support: education, experience, projects, certifications, skills, preferred roles, locations, and links. Select the verification checkbox after reviewing the information.

## 2. Build portfolio evidence

Open **Portfolio Studio**. Add a completed project, state its origin honestly, and include architecture, implementation, security, tests, monitoring, troubleshooting, rollback, outcome, and evidence. The secret scanner blocks likely credentials. Instructor or owner approval is needed before publication.

## 3. Generate a portfolio

Select a general or job-specific target and generate the portfolio. The bot creates a static HTML website, PDF summary, and GitHub-ready ZIP. Review all files before publishing them to a public host or repository.

## 4. Analyze jobs

Paste a job description or configure a permitted public source. Review the score, matched requirements, missing requirements, and recommendation. A high match score is advisory rather than a guarantee of selection.

## 5. Generate the job-specific resume

Select a job and optional portfolio build. The bot reorders and emphasizes verified material. It does not add missing skills. Review the change summary and all generated statements before submitting.

## 6. Prepare and send an email application

Enter the recruiter address, subject, and message. The bot validates attachments and creates an EML preview. Gmail integration uses OAuth; the bot never asks for your Gmail password. Sending requires typing the exact word `SEND` for the prepared draft.

## 7. Portal applications

For portals with official submission support, follow the permitted workflow. For restricted portals, use assisted mode: the bot prepares the resume, portfolio, and suggested answers, then opens or records the application while you perform the final portal submission.

## 8. Prepare for the employer interview

Select the applied job and generate the interview pack. The bot labels whether information comes from the job description, verified candidate material, public company information, reported candidate experience, or general role inference. Use the pack to launch the complete recruitment simulator.
