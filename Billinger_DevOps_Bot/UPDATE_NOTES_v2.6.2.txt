Billinger Bot v2.6.2 — Four-Provider, High-Contrast and Timeout Maintenance Release

- Gemini, OpenAI, Groq and OpenRouter display together in one row on normal laptop and desktop screens.
- Medium screens use a 2x2 layout and mobile screens use one card per row.
- Added a persistent four-provider overview and direct jump buttons.
- Changed light-grey dashboard copy, labels, helper text and placeholders to darker high-contrast text.
- Increased default AI timeout to 120 seconds and supports up to 300 seconds.
- Provider connection tests use a small response, a 120-second minimum read window and low/no reasoning where supported.
- Timeout errors now distinguish response delay from network, authentication, quota and invalid-model failures.
