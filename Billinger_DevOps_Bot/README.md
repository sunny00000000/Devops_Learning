# Billinger DevOps Learning Bot v2.9.0 — 4K Precision Polish Edition

A lightweight, portable Windows DevOps learning, practice, institute, portfolio, career, recruitment, and adaptive-AI platform for up to 10 local student profiles.

## Final readiness workflow

A fresh student profile is directed to **Start Readiness** before normal learning. The launch gate covers:

- Administrator protection.
- Target role and daily study time.
- A balanced 20-question baseline assessment.
- Verified learning-library availability.
- Optional read-only local tool detection.
- Optional online-AI provider health.
- Verified backup integrity.
- The first personalized daily plan.

Online AI and external DevOps tools are optional; the verified local course works without them.

## Evidence-based mastery

Each of the 21 DevOps domains has nine independent graduation gates:

1. All lessons completed.
2. All guided labs passed.
3. Knowledge test score of at least 85%.
4. Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Master practical exams at 80% or higher.
5. All company tickets passed.
6. Production incident score of at least 78%.
7. Enterprise capstone score of at least 82%.
8. Mock interview score of at least 80%.
9. Verified portfolio evidence score of at least 80%.

Opening a lesson is therefore not treated as mastery.

## Start

1. Extract the ZIP into a new folder.
2. Double-click `Start_Billinger_Bot.bat`.
3. Complete **Start Readiness**.
4. Begin with the recommended foundation domain.

The launcher uses a bundled portable runtime when present, otherwise installed Python, and can perform a one-time portable-runtime setup when required.
